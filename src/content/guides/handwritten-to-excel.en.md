The sheet is in your hand and the gradebook is on your device: two paths separate them — smart handwriting reading or fast manual entry, and a review screen that puts every mark in its place before export.

## 1. Pick your path

- **Scan with the camera**: photograph the handwritten sheet and the marks are extracted automatically. Needs an internet connection, and a scan credit is spent only after a successful read.
- **Manual entry**: from the manual entry screen pick your class, record absences, then type the marks on the keypad — always free, never spends a credit.

## 2. Review before saving

Either way, results pass through the review screen: every line matched to a student by **matricule**, not by name — a name can repeat within the same class. Fix any misread value, complete what the reading skipped; nothing is written to your gradebook until you confirm.

## 3. Export to Excel

After saving, the marks live in your gradebook — export from there:

- **Updated Excel file** — same student columns and registration numbers, ready to open in Excel.
- **The digitization file (ملف الرقمنة)** — the digital copy of your marks, alongside a PDF report for your class.

An export costs one credit, and credits never expire.

## 4. Tips for a clean read

- One sheet per photo, flat and evenly lit without shadows.
- Keep the registration-number column inside the frame at all times.
- Review first the lines holding implausible values (negative, above the limit, empty).

## 5. After export

Keep a copy of the file in your class folder and update it after every period. Your next task starts from the same gradebook: edit any mark by tapping it, and export again when the period ends.
