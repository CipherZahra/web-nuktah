The scan is where Nuktah does the heavy lifting — three conditions and a review step separate a clean read from a retake.

## 1. Prepare the sheet

- Lay the sheet flat — folds and curled corners hide rows.
- Even light, with no shadow of your phone falling on the page.
- Keep the whole page in frame, especially the matricule column.

## 2. One sheet per photo

Photograph a single sheet at a time. The reader expects one grid of rows; overlapping pages produce mixed results that take longer to fix than to retake.

## 3. Reading happens online

The photo is read by the OCR service, so the phone needs an internet connection at that moment. **A scan credit is only spent after a successful read** — never when you press the shutter, and never for a failed attempt.

## 4. The review screen is not optional

Before anything is saved, every detected row appears matched to a student by matricule. This is where you correct a misread number, or fill in a row the reader skipped. Nothing is written to your class until you confirm.

## 5. When a row comes out wrong

First check the photo conditions — flat sheet, good light — and rescan if needed. If only a few values are off, correct them by hand on the review screen; the save still goes through in seconds.

## 6. After saving

The grades land in the class grade book against each student’s ID. From there you can edit any grade by tapping it, and export the class report as a PDF or an updated Excel workbook.
