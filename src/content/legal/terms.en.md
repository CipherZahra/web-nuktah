## About the service

Nuktah is a teacher's grade-management assistant: import an Excel workbook, scan grade sheets, and generate reports. The app is provided “as-is”.

## Your use of data

You are responsible for the data you enter or scan. Use the app only with student data you are authorized to handle in your professional role.

## Accuracy — your responsibility

The app helps read grades, but you **must review every grade on the review screen before saving**. The app is not an official grade record.

## Nuktah AI

Grade-sheet photos are sent to the Nuktah AI service to read grades after your consent. See the [Privacy Policy](/en/privacy) for details.

## Credits and payments

- Every account starts with free credit: 2 scans and 1 workbook import.
- On the free plan, each scan, import, or report export uses 1 credit; viewing, manual entry, and sharing are free.
- **Pro** subscribers get unlimited scans and imports, while report exports use 1 credit on every plan.
- You can earn extra credit by watching an optional rewarded ad, up to 2 credits per day.
- Credits never expire. Purchases are processed via Chargily and subject to its terms.
- Prices are shown in Algerian dinar (DZD).
