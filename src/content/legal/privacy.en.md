In Nuktah, we respect your privacy and work to protect your personal data. This policy explains how data is collected, used, stored, and shared, and the choices available to you regarding your data when you use the app and our website.

## 1. The data we process

### Account data

When you create an account, your email address is processed to manage the account and provide the related services. Account data is stored using the technical infrastructure relied on to operate the service.

### Educational data

Educational data may include students' names, their registration numbers, their grades, and information imported from Excel workbooks or entered while using the app.

Under the app's current way of working, this data is kept on your device and is not uploaded to Nuktah servers as part of ordinary local storage. Processing may differ when you use features that require sending data to an external service, as described in this policy.

You are responsible for confirming that you have the necessary authority to handle the educational data you enter or process.

### Grade-sheet photos

When you use the Nuktah AI feature, and after your consent inside the app, a photo of the grade sheet is sent to an artificial-intelligence service to process the image and extract the grades.

Under the current mechanism, Nuktah does not store scanned images on its servers or with the hosting provider. Images and data sent to the processing provider may be subject to that provider's own terms and policies, including provisions on data retention.

We advise you to review the image and confirm it is appropriate to send before starting processing.

### Technical data and error logs

Limited technical information may be processed, such as the number of tokens processed during scans, error logs, and crash diagnostics, to improve the service's stability and performance.

### Anti-abuse data

When an account is created, information such as IP address, browser or device information, and a normalized form of your email address may be processed to help prevent automated sign-ups and abuse of the service.

These records may be retained for up to 12 months, depending on the purpose for which they were collected and the retention mechanisms relied on.

## 2. How data is used

Data is used for the following purposes:

- Creating the account and managing it.
- Providing the app's features and handling user requests.
- Extracting grades from images when you request the scan feature.
- Processing payments and managing transaction records when you use purchase features.
- Detecting errors and technical issues and improving the service's reliability.
- Limiting abuse and protecting accounts and the service.
- Responding to support requests and meeting applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell users' personal data.

## 3. Data we do not collect

We do not collect your geolocation or your contacts list as part of the features described in this policy.

Some external services may process the technical information needed to provide their features, depending on the integration used and its settings and policies.

## 4. Advertising

The app may show banner ads, interstitial ads, and rewarded ads.

Watching a rewarded ad is optional and grants additional credit according to the limits and terms shown inside the app.

Advertising identifiers and information about interaction with ads may be collected or processed according to your device settings and the ad provider's policies. You can manage some advertising options from your device settings where those options are available.

## 5. Payments

Purchases are processed through the payment provider available inside the service.

Bank-card data is processed by the payment provider under the relied-on integration mechanism, and bank-card numbers do not pass through Nuktah servers under the current design.

Nuktah may keep the information needed to manage transactions, such as the order reference, the amount, the payment status, and the credit linked to the operation.

The processing of payment data is also subject to the terms and policies of the relevant payment provider.

## 6. Sharing data and service providers

The data that is necessary may be shared with the service providers who help operate the app and provide its features, limited to what is needed for the relevant purpose.

The categories of services used include:

- Account and database hosting: to operate accounts and store the data linked to them.
- AI image processing: to extract grades from grade-sheet photos when the user requests it.
- Advertising services: to show ads and manage interaction with rewarded ads.
- Payment processing: to complete transactions and confirm their status.
- Technical error monitoring: to diagnose faults and improve the service's stability.

## 7. Data retention

We keep data for as long as needed to provide the service and fulfil the purposes described in this policy, or for as long as legal obligations or security requirements demand, depending on the case.

Retention periods differ by data type and by the purpose of processing it. Removing data from your device does not necessarily delete any separate records held by external service providers.

## 8. Deleting your account and data

You can request the deletion of your account from inside the app via the following path:

Profile → Delete account

After you confirm the deletion, the account and the data linked to it are removed from our systems under the approved deletion mechanism. Local data linked to the app is also deleted from your device as the deletion feature is implemented.

Some records may remain with service providers, or records that must be kept by law or for legitimate security purposes, within what is permitted.

If you cannot access the app, you can request account deletion through the account-deletion form available on our website, or contact us at:

**support@nuktah.app**

Please send the request from the email address linked to the account and write "Delete account" in the subject line.

## 9. Data security

We use encrypted HTTPS connections to transmit data between the app and the services it connects to, and we work to apply appropriate technical and organizational measures to limit unauthorized access to data.

That said, absolute security cannot be guaranteed for any electronic system or means of transmitting data.

## 10. Your rights

Under Algerian Law No. 18-07 on the protection of natural persons in the processing of personal data, and depending on what applies to your situation, you may have the right to:

- Access your personal data.
- Request correction of inaccurate data.
- Request deletion of the data under the legal conditions.
- Ask about how your data is processed.

To exercise your rights or to raise any question related to privacy, contact us at:

**support@nuktah.app**

## 11. Privacy of children and students

The Nuktah app is designed for professional use by teachers and is not directed at children.

## 12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app's features change, or the data-processing methods, or legal requirements.

We will publish the updated version on the Nuktah website and update the last-updated date. We recommend reviewing this page regularly.
